General

Closing the window keeps Launchpad in the system tray. Use the tray icon's Quit to exit.
Dropped files and folders are kept as copies, so they still open if the original moves. Programs and shortcuts are always added as links. Hold Alt while dropping to add a link instead.

Grid placer
Opens a grid over the screen to size the window you're using: drag across cells, or use the arrow keys (Shift+arrows to resize) and Enter. Needs the window organizer.

Window organizer

Click a zone in one of the panel's layouts to move the window you're using there. Right-click an open window on the taskbar for the same layouts. While the panel is on, it also carries Launchpad's top bar on the screen Launchpad is on, and follows the top bar look. Make your own with Layouts.
Organizer panel
Auto-hide brings the panel down when the pointer rests against the top edge. Fullscreen apps cover it, apart from Launchpad itself.
Drop the window on a zone in the layouts that appear. Press Esc or move away to cancel.

Backup & updates

Backup
Includes your apps, layouts, and the Media library and players. Artwork isn't included.
Repair Launchpad
Downloads this version's installer from its GitHub release and runs it, like an update. Your apps and settings are kept. Launchpad restarts when it's done.

Danger zone

Reset Launchpad
Removes your apps, islands, icons, stored files, layouts, scenes and settings, and restarts Launchpad as a first install. Your Media library, players and artwork are kept.

Window layouts

Scenes

A scene remembers where your app windows are, on every screen. Apply it to put each of those windows that's open back in place. Save one from an island (right-click it) and the island can open as that scene, starting whichever of its apps aren't running.

No scenes yet. Arrange your windows, then save them with the button below or the scenes button on the panel.

Reset Launchpad

This deletes:

  • Your apps, islands, pins and their icons
  • Files stored in Launchpad (the originals aren't touched)
  • Your window layouts and scenes
  • All settings, theme and taskbar look

Your Media library, players and artwork are kept.

A copy of what's deleted is saved as pre-reset-…json in Launchpad's data folder first. It doesn't hold stored files, so export a backup if you have any.
